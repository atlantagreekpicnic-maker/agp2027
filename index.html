Know before you go

What can't I bring?+

No weapons, illegal substances, large bags or backpacks, professional cameras, outside food or drinks, or personal tents. Media must be vetted in advance.

Is there food and drink on site?+

Yes. A range of food vendors, including options for dietary restrictions, plus multiple bars and VIP service.

Is AGP accessible?+

Yes. Accessible parking, restrooms and seating areas are available, and we can accommodate you through any VIP entrance.

Can I leave and come back?+

No. Re-entry is not allowed.

How It Works

Serve. Scan. Win.

01

Apply & lock your booth

Apply as a food or beverage vendor for AGP 2027. Every approved food and beverage booth is entered in Taste of AGP.

02

Serve the yard

Set up at Atlantic Station on picnic day and put your signature item in front of the crowd.

03

The crowd votes

Each booth gets its own QR code. Attendees scan it and vote by category, right from their phones. No app to download.

04

Winners get crowned

Category winners are announced and carry the Taste of AGP title for the year.

Vendor questions

Who can enter Taste of AGP?+

Food and beverage vendors with an approved booth at AGP 2027.

How does voting work?+

Every competing booth displays a QR code. Attendees scan it on site and vote by category.

Is it a separate event?+

No. Taste of AGP is built into the main AGP weekend. You serve the picnic crowd as usual, and the competition runs alongside.
